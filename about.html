Our story

Soukaina,
fashion creator.

Tendance by Souka began the way most good things do — with one person noticing what other people were missing. Soukaina had spent years with an instinct for the piece that completes an outfit: the dress with the right drop, the shoe that changes the line of everything above it.

What started as styling friends and posting her finds turned into a small boutique on Avenue Mohamed Diouri in Kénitra, and a community of over 7,600 women who trust her taste. The shop is deliberately edited. If it isn't elegant, or it isn't interesting, it doesn't go on the rail.

Today she works the same way she always has: one conversation at a time, by message, helping women find the pieces that feel like them — whether they're ten minutes away or on another continent.

What we stand for

Four things
we never trade.

They're not slogans on a wall — they're the rules that decide what makes it into the shop.

01

Élégance

Refinement over trend. We look for clean lines, beautiful fabric and a fit that flatters — pieces that still feel right next season.

02

Creativity

A curated shop should surprise you. Mixed textures, unexpected colour, silhouettes you won't find on every high street corner.

03

Personal care

You are never just an order number. Every message is answered by Soukaina, and every parcel is packed by hand.

04

Quality curation

Small quantities, chosen deliberately. If we wouldn't wear it, we won't sell it — it's as simple as that.
